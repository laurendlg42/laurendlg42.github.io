Technical Mastery Drills

The Power Progression

  1. Static Balance: Hold the starting position for 10 seconds.
  2. Linear Drive: Focus on explosive force along the X-axis.
  3. Torque Generation: Rotate the hips before the shoulders.
  4. Release Extension: Ensure maximum lever length at the finish.

Drill Frequency

For optimal mechanical retention, we recommend:

  • 3 Sessions per week.
  • 50 Repetitions per phase.
  • Video analysis after every 10 reps.
